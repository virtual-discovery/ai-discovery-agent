## Construction

The scripts are in `benchmarks/construction/li3ps4-superionic-transport/`:

- `fetch.sh` refetches the zip, checks its md5, extracts the members above and checks their md5.
- `extract.py` reads the members into `pool.json` without changing any value:
  - Each run is keyed by its nominal temperature; the archive stores the run's mean temperature, which is served alongside.
  - Heat capacities are keyed by the two consecutive simulated temperatures of the finite difference rather than by their midpoint.
  - The Li MSD (65,400 points at 10 fs) is decimated to every 10 fs up to 2 ps, every 0.1 ps up to 20 ps and every 1 ps after that.
  - The σ(τ) breakdown is decimated to every 20 fs.
  - The order-parameter samples are decimated to every 7th sample, and the reference clusters are reduced to their mean and standard deviation.
- `make_task.py` writes the catalog, the lab data, the private targets and the ablation values.

Two further choices:

- **β series cut at 625 K.** The β-initialised conductivities are served only up to 625 K. At 650–1150 K the archived r2SCAN-β and PBE0-β series coincide to about 0.1 % (z ≈ 0.01 against block errors of about 1 %), so at least one of them is mislabelled in the archive. Below 650 K they differ and match the paper's Table IV values at 500 K.
- **Labels from the paper.** Three labels are not in the archive's READMEs and are taken from the paper's text: the Li MSD is the ML-PBE0 run started from γ at 750 K ("the simulation at 750 K of Fig. 6"), the Fig. 4 order parameters are ML-PBE0 runs started from γ, and the Fig. 9 correlations are 190 ps of an α-phase run at 650 K. The Li MSD label is consistent with the archived ML-PBE0 γ 750 K Nernst–Einstein conductivity for a plausible NpT volume of about 16,700 Å³. The Fig. 7 series (heat capacity, PS4 angle fluctuation, rotational diffusion, P diffusion) and the σ(τ) breakdown are served without an initial-phase or condition label, as in the archive.

## What is hidden

- **Withheld runs.** Both conductivity estimators are withheld for PBEsol/γ/625 K, PBE0/γ/750 K and r2SCAN/γ/800 K: the first simulated temperature above each reference's transition. Their archived Green–Kubo values, and the archived Haven ratio of the PBE0 run, grade the submission.
- **Transition temperatures** of PBE0 and r2SCAN: the lower heat-capacity peak, below the onset of P diffusion. For every reference it coincides with the largest jump of the γ-started Nernst–Einstein conductivity and with the step of the PS4 angle fluctuation (asserted in `make_task.py`).
- **Activation energies** of σ (Green–Kubo, σT Arrhenius, block-error weights) for the PBE0 γ-started runs:
  - below the transition, over the resolved points at 600–725 K;
  - above it, from 750 to 1000 K.
- **Li–S fraction:** the matrix-frame Li–S term over the total, averaged over τ = 2–8 ps.
- **Hop-to-flip rate ratio at 800 K:**
  - D_Li comes from the archived PBE0 γ Nernst–Einstein conductivity and the ideal γ cell volume;
  - the hop rate is 6D/(2.5 Å)²;
  - the flip rate is 4D_ω/φ², with φ = 70.5°.

Tolerances were set between the spread of the defensible analyses in `solution/ablations.py` and the nearest plausible wrong path. The paper's tabulated activation energies are fitted to Nernst–Einstein conductivities; the graded activation energies here are Green–Kubo values from the archive and differ from them.

## Scope

This is a retrospective analysis of released ML-MD results, not new simulation. All values are the authors' block-averaged estimates.
