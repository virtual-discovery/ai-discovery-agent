# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Uses the spinful C3z symmetry indicator exp(2πiC/3) = −ξ_γ ξ_κ ξ_κ′ for the K-valley spin-up states only, notes that the spin-down (K′) partner of each doublet carries the opposite Chern number, and states that C is fixed only modulo 3 (checked against a Berry-curvature calculation where one is available).
2. Justifies the κ′ eigenvalue from data or symmetry (equal to that at κ, not its complex conjugate) rather than assuming it.
3. Connects each Chern change to band inversions at specific momenta: for tWSe2 between 1.70° and 1.54°, bands 1–2 at γ and bands 2–3 at κ/κ′; for tMoTe2 between 3.15° and 2.14°, bands 2–3 at γ; and notes the multiple C = +1 bands of tMoTe2 near 2° and the (−1, −1) pair of 1.25° tWSe2 (same Chern numbers at ν = −1 and −3, consistent with the 1.23° experiment).
4. Identifies which continuum parameter set reproduces the 3.89° DFT bands by a quantitative comparison, and explains that the other (local-stacking) set gives the opposite top-band Chern number because its layer-pseudospin skyrmion has reversed poles (opposite Δ_z at XM and MX).
5. Shows that a twist-independent continuum model (set P at 2.14°) does not reproduce the DFT Chern evolution, so the change is not captured by a fixed local-stacking-type model.
6. Locates XM and MX in the Hartree maps from the relaxed structures and reports the sign reversal of Δv_H at XM between 3.15° and 1.70°, together with the growth of the flipped region at smaller angles.
7. Explains that a first-star expansion fails at small angles (first-star part nearly zero at 1.70° while the full drop is large; higher harmonics; six-petal MM structure), and that the archived Fourier-amplitude file cannot show the sign change because it holds magnitudes only.
8. Attributes the flip to the competition between the out-of-plane moiré ferroelectric polarization (local stacking, XM vs MX) and the relaxation-induced in-plane piezoelectric charge (strain gradients concentrated at domain walls at small angles), and notes that tMoTe2 has no archived Hartree data, so its mechanism is inferred by analogy.
