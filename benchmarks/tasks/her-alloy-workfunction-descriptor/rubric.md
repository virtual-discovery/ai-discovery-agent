# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Explains that on the alloy the slope of the differential adsorption energy along the filling path mixes H-H repulsion with site heterogeneity (strong hollows fill first). Removes each added site's own dilute energy to isolate the interaction, gives the resulting shift (about 0.2 eV from 1/9 to 1 ML), and notes the large spread between decorations.
2. States the free-energy convention: Delta G_H = Delta E_H + about 0.24 eV (ZPE and entropy), so the dilute textbook optimum is Delta E_H = -0.24 eV. Shows that the activity-derived volcano centre (about -0.44 eV) is shifted from it by roughly the coverage shift, and that a fixed -0.24 eV centre fits the measured currents much worse.
3. Averages the rate over each spot's site-energy distribution rather than evaluating it at the mean energy. Notes that the spot-mean energies lie on one side of the optimum, so a mean-energy volcano cannot locate the peak.
4. Identifies mass-transport saturation at high overpotential (currents near 2 A/cm^2 at -300/-400 mV) and includes a limiting current in the model.
5. Recognises that the prefactor and the limiting current differ strongly between libraries. Handles this with library-specific parameters, calibrates Lib-3 on purchased spots, and does not transfer absolute currents directly.
6. Tests the work function fairly: compares a work-function-only fit with a fit that also contains the coverage-corrected binding term, with library-specific offsets and a transfer or held-out check. Concludes that alpha_Phi largely collapses (WF-only about 0.18 vs about 0.06 conditional, and not stable between libraries) because Phi tracks the Pd/Pt content.
7. Interprets a work-function term physically (interfacial field, charge transfer, double layer) and states that these data cannot separate it from composition or binding.
8. Identifies Pd-rich compositions with some Pt as the most active region at realistic coverage, using the pure-metal anchors (Pd about -0.50 eV, Pt about -0.33 eV dilute), and explains why dilute reasoning would wrongly favour Pt.
