# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Compares the polymorphs with a model of positions, intensities and polarization selection rules, not by nearest-line position matching (which favours the line-dense monoclinic C2/c record). States why the tetragonal P4_12_12 record, whose strong lines nearly coincide with the orthorhombic P2_12_12_1 ones, is rejected (split doublets, low-frequency mode positions, in-plane anisotropy of the polarization patterns).
2. Notes that the Stokes and anti-Stokes lines (or the notch edges) are not symmetric about zero on the spectrometer axis, attributes this to a calibration offset of the laser line rather than to the sample, and fits it separately from the multiplicative scale s.
3. Uses the full Raman-tensor rotation Q R Q^T with the Bose–Einstein (n+1)/ω and scattered-photon factors, and sums degenerate partners where they occur.
4. Explains why polarizer/analyzer settings along the lab axes alone leave a mirror-related orientation that fits equally well, and shows how rotated settings remove it. Also notes that face-A backscattering cannot distinguish a 180° turn about the face normal, and that the face-B quantities do not depend on it.
5. Treats LO–TO mixing of polar modes for the phonon wavevector along the face normal (Born charges and ε∞): it explains the extra lines that are absent from the database's TO list, and the face-B phonon is computed for q along n_B, not taken from the TO list.
6. Notices that the archived Born charges list only the symmetry-independent atoms, and expands them with the space-group operations, rotating each tensor; it checks the acoustic sum rule.
7. Identifies the minority phase from weak lines whose polarization does not follow the single-crystal selection rules (orientation-averaged inclusions), and distinguishes them from LO-mixed lines of the main phase.
8. States uncertainties and limits: the neglected electro-optic contribution to LO Raman tensors, that absolute scattering efficiencies and the minority volume fraction are only roughly constrained, and that the database quality flags (e.g. path instabilities) do not by themselves decide the phase.
