# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. States the conventions: the archived TB2J energy counts ordered pairs with unit vectors; the spin-1/2 chain coupling is −8 times the TB2J value; magnon energies for spin length S scale as 1/S; wave vectors are in reciprocal-lattice units of the conventional cell, with sign and integer shifts equivalent.
2. Explains the self-consistency loop and applies it: both FM references are unstable (negative bosonic-matrix eigenvalues), with k_min near (1/2, 0, 1/2) with the spin-ligand correction and near (0, 1/2, 0) without it. The 2×1×2 AFM reference with the correction is positive semi-definite over the whole zone, with only the Goldstone zero, and its moment pattern is the optimum of its own exchange. It is therefore converged, with commensurate collinear AF1 order at (1/2, 0, ±1/2).
3. Quantifies the spin-ligand correction: without it the FM-reference chain coupling is about 45 % stronger (TB2J −21.3 against −14.6 meV) and k_min moves to (0, 1/2, 0). Identifies the uncorrected AFM calculation as unconverged and internally inconsistent: its structure has two inequivalent Cu sites (different Cu–O bond lengths and moment sizes), its nominally equivalent bonds differ by tens of meV, and it is unstable at its own reference.
4. Recognises reference-state dependence, not noise, in the AFM-reference list. The chain coupling is about 11 % stronger than in the FM reference. Bonds equivalent by crystal symmetry differ systematically with the relative orientation of their spins: antiparallel pairs are more antiferromagnetic by up to about 1 meV. Symmetrising the list lowers the AF1 stabilisation by about 4 meV/Cu and the magnon energies by about 14 %, and it shifts the Luttinger–Tisza optimum to a slightly incommensurate spiral.
5. Establishes the exchange range needed: k_min requires couplings beyond about 8 Å (a 6 Å cutoff moves it by about 0.01 r.l.u.), with an explicit convergence test under the budget.
6. Solves the spin waves as the paraunitary (g·h, Colpa) problem on the 16-site AF1 cell. Distinguishes the eigenvalues of h(k), which are what the authors' dispersion files contain, from magnon energies, and validates the input against the archived h(k), for example at Γ.
7. Discusses the limits of the isotropic model. The converged isotropic model selects commensurate collinear AF1, the experimental low-temperature phase. The incommensurate multiferroic AF2 spiral needs anisotropic exchange, Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya interaction or thermal fluctuations, which these isotropic data cannot address. Any spiral–AF1 energy difference of order 0.1 meV/Cu lies below the resolution of the method. A Néel temperature is not supported: mean field overestimates it for this quasi-one-dimensional chain magnet, and the reference-dependent exchange is not valid far from AF1.
