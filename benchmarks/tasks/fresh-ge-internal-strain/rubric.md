# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Explains why a shear strain produces forces on the sublattices of the diamond structure (neither atom is a centre of inversion; the Td site symmetry allows a coupling between the shear ε4 and the relative displacement u_x, while ε1–ε3 produce none), and defines the internal-strain coupling Λ (force per unit strain), the zone-centre force constant Φ of the relative coordinate and the Kleinman parameter ζ.
2. Derives C44 = C44⁰ − Λ²/(Ω Φ) and ζ = 4Λ/(a Φ) (or equivalent forms in the stated conventions) and checks them numerically against the directly computed relaxed shear stress or energy and the relaxed displacement; uses the outcome to decide between "internal relaxation is negligible", "internal relaxation is large" and "the apparent effect is a numerical artefact".
3. Identifies that PBE Ge without spin–orbit coupling has an inverted (negative) gap at Γ at its own a0 (the s-like Γ2′ level lies below the p-like Γ25′ triplet; a shifted mesh hides this and a Γ-containing mesh with fixed occupations fills part of a degenerate level), that the gap opens under compression, and that at 6 GPa the internal-strain force still depends on sampling the small direct gap at Γ: it converges on Γ-containing meshes but is too small on offset meshes that converge the energy, pressure and clamped stress. Chooses mesh and occupations from the convergence of the graded quantities themselves (or the Maxwell relation Ω ∂σ_yz/∂u_x = Λ), and notes that wide smearing softens the optical force constant.
4. Shows that the pressure of this 3d-in-valence ultrasoft pseudopotential converges more slowly with the cutoff than total energies (Pulay stress: about 1.4 GPa at 35 Ry, 0.15 GPa at 45 Ry), so lattice parameters are taken from converged pressures; and treats the 6 GPa pre-stress consistently (stress–strain coefficients, or an energy route that removes the −P ΔV work).
5. Checks that relaxed geometries are actually relaxed: compares the residual forces of a relaxation with the clamped-ion forces (a loose force threshold at percent-level strain stops the relaxation early and biases ζ low and C44 high), or locates the zero-force displacement by interpolation.
6. Obtains the Raman frequency with the reduced mass M/2 of the relative coordinate and cancels the cubic anharmonic term (which is allowed along [111] by the Td symmetry) with ± displacements or a displacement along a cube axis; obtains the Grüneisen parameter from frequencies computed with identical settings at volumes bracketing a0.
7. Compares with experiment (ambient: a ≈ 5.658 Å, C44 ≈ 67 GPa, ζ ≈ 0.44–0.54, ω ≈ 301 cm⁻¹ rising by ≈ 4 cm⁻¹/GPa), attributes the protocol's softness mainly to the PBE overestimate of the lattice parameter, and states the limits: static lattice, no spin–orbit coupling (which also affects the Γ ordering), harmonic small-strain limit, one functional and pseudopotential (with the size of the frozen-3d-core difference if measured); the zero-pressure internal-strain values of this protocol are not established by these calculations.
