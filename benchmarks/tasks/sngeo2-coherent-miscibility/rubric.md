# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Identifies the alloy as clustering-type: every released ordering and every SQS has a positive formation energy (no ordered ground state), so the incoherent alloy has a miscibility gap rather than an order–disorder transition; ordering and "small mixing enthalpy" are ruled out with numbers (mixing enthalpy about 0.11 eV/cation at x = 0.5).
2. Establishes the normalisation of the released SQS energies with evidence: the file mixes end-member total energies with formation energies, and the intermediate values are per rutile primitive cell (2 cations), shown by an independent route (the HSE-setting PBE totals or the orderings/cluster expansion), not assumed.
3. Detects and excludes the three r2SCAN SQS that did not reach their equilibrium volume (volume about 2.4 % below the Vegard line and the r2SCAN/PBE volume ratio of their identical PBE decorations, energies about 20 meV/cation too high), and states that keeping them raises the r2SCAN coherent Tc to about 900 K and shifts its maximum to Ge-rich x.
4. Gives the incoherent critical temperature with its method, notes that the ideal-entropy (mean-field) estimate is an upper bound, that short-range order from a cluster expansion with Monte Carlo lowers it by roughly 10 %, and that the cluster-expansion value depends on truncation and validation; in every case it lies far above the growth temperatures, so the films are not equilibrium solid solutions.
5. Computes Δe_cs by holding both in-plane lattice parameters of each end member at the homogeneous alloy's values and relaxing the out-of-plane parameter (minimum of each scan), compares all three released planes, and explains why (001) is the habit plane (the larger c-axis mismatch relaxes out of plane); notes that the archive's "101" dataset strains a2 − a3 and c, i.e. a (110)-type plane in the standard setting.
6. Concludes that coherency strain suppresses the gap to a coherent spinodal Tc below or near the growth temperatures, so coherent epitaxial films can stay homogeneous and are metastable; and rules out a uniform substrate strain (linear in x, no change of curvature) and configurational entropy alone as the explanation.
7. Discusses functional sensitivity (r2SCAN raises the mixing enthalpy and the strain energy, leaving the coherent Tc similar or lower) and keeps each functional's data separate.
8. States the limits: vibrational entropy, interface and gradient energies, dislocations or loss of coherency, kinetics and amorphisation are not in the model, and the coherent boundaries depend on the assumed habit plane imposed by the substrate.
