# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Establishes the pristine electron counts from the archive (states below the archived Fermi level, or the DFT valence count minus the excluded bands), finds 28/31/34 rather than the nominal 27/30/33, and attributes the extra electron to Cs 6s charge donated into the d-p manifold.
2. Identifies which CsCr3Bi5 folder has which lattice from the .wout lattice vectors (CrBi: a = 5.49 Å, the CsCr3Sb5 lattice; CrBi_9.2: a = 5.79 Å, the CsTi3Bi5 lattice), and notes that the folder name does not encode this.
3. Derives the orbital names from the archive's projection definitions (order dxy, dyz, dz2, dxz, dx2-y2 per site, site-local frames with x radial), with a consistency check such as the onsite Ag mixing, and names dxz as the most renormalized orbital in that frame (equivalently the B3g-type orbital of a frame with x and y exchanged).
4. Separates filling from kinetic energy with counterfactual evaluations (Ti Hamiltonian at the Cr count, Cr Hamiltonian at the Ti count), shows that the two one-at-a-time paths disagree strongly, and concludes that the two effects act synergistically rather than additively; does not rely on a cross-compound correlation.
5. Quantifies the kinetic-energy reduction with σ (full PDOS second moment, including d-p hybridization) and links the smallest σ to the most renormalized orbital and to the compound trend Ti > V > Cr and Sb > Bi.
6. Uses the J dependence at fixed U to identify Hund's-metal physics (Z̄ recovers strongly at J = 0), with occupations near half filling per orbital, rather than attributing the correlations to U alone.
7. Separates the Bi effect into chemistry (Bi at the CsCr3Sb5 lattice) and lattice expansion (CsTi3Bi5 lattice), notes the models have no spin-orbit coupling so SOC cannot be the cause, and reports that CsCr3Bi5 has no metallic SSMF solution at U = 6 eV (the most correlated member, at or beyond the Mott boundary).
8. States limits: SSMF approximations, U and J not computed, no structure known for CsCr3Bi5, no SOC, magnetism, CDW or ARPES/optical comparison in these data.
