# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Assigns each archive folder its doping from the run's own input (tot_charge, with the Fermi energy or electron count as a cross-check), and notes that the folders do not follow the order of the README's doping list (folder 5 is x = 0.065, folder 6 is x = 0; folders 0–4 are 0.02–0.10).
2. Identifies the instability: for x ≤ 0.06 a single mode at H = (1/3, 1/3, 1/2) is imaginary (−32 cm⁻¹ at x = 0, −8 cm⁻¹ at x = 0.06) and hardens with doping. Locates x_c ≈ 0.064 by interpolating ω² (analytic across a displacive instability), not ω, and states that the bracketing x = 0.065 and 0.08 phonons were not available.
3. States that below x_c the harmonic α²F, the coupling (λ = 2.10 at x = 0 against 1.27 at x = 0.10) and any Tc describe a structure that is not stable, so they are not predictions for lightly doped BaB2. Notes that N(E_F) changes by only about 5% across the series, so a rigid-band change of N(E_F) does not explain the fall of λ; the soft branch does.
4. Explains the MgB2-like mechanism (holes in boron σ bands, cylinders along Γ–A, coupled to in-plane B bond-stretching modes) and the two-gap structure: about 11.6 and 2.3 meV at 25 K for x = 0.065, ratio ≈ 5. Explains that the anisotropic Tc (≈ 62 K) is about twice the isotropic one at the same μ* (≈ 34 K at μ* = 0.13), because the strongly coupled σ states are not averaged with the weakly coupled π states.
5. Describes how the isotropic Tc was obtained and checked. The archived logs use μ* = 0.13 and stop at 30 K, below Tc for most dopings, and the x = 0.08 log ends in NaN and "Convergence was not reached" at 30 K. The rerun used μ* = 0.10 at a 500 meV cutoff and was validated against the archived gaps. Close to Tc the iterations converge slowly, so unconverged finite gaps were not taken as superconductivity. Notes that Allen–Dynes underestimates the strong-coupling Eliashberg Tc.
6. Obtains the anisotropic Tc by extrapolating the imaginary-axis gaps (Δ² or the free energy) beyond the last archived temperature (60 K, gap still open). Notes that the real-axis (Padé) distributions contain negative gaps from about 30 K upwards and are not used for the gap structure.
7. On realisability: shows that the `331` supercell, described as homogeneously 1-hole doped, is undoped (144 electrons, the undoped Fermi energy 11.3085 eV and cell, folded-H mode −32 cm⁻¹ as in the undoped primitive cell). States that homogeneous doping of 1/9 h/f.u. is stable (≈ +25 cm⁻¹), while the Cs-substituted cell remains unstable at the folded H point (−39.5 and −25.5 cm⁻¹), so Cs substitution does not reproduce homogeneous doping.
8. States uncertainties and limits: μ* (Tc changes by about 8% between 0.10 and 0.13); k/q-grid convergence untested; x_c resolved only to the spacing of the purchasable dopings; the anisotropic Tc extrapolated from 60 K; the α²F smearing columns change λ by less than 0.3% near threshold.
