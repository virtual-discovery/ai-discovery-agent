# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Identifies critical magnetic scattering around (0 0 3) that peaks at T_C (about 178 K), falls slowly above T_C and vanishes within roughly 10 K below it. Notes that the narrow scans alone leave a gap between 140 and 178 K, which the 165 K wide-scan data fill. Does not treat the scattering as symmetric in |T − T_C|.
2. Treats the background as temperature dependent (it rises smoothly with T at both reflections) and chooses references that bracket the predicted temperature, rather than using 1.5 K or 300 K data as a fixed background. Does not use Bragg-peak intensities for the magnetic structure: they change non-monotonically even between 180 and 300 K, above T_C.
3. Uses the polarisation factor: at (0 0 3), Q ∥ c, so only in-plane spin components scatter, and the critical signal therefore shows critical in-plane ferromagnetic fluctuations, which a strong Ising (single-ion) easy axis would suppress.
4. Compares (1 0 1) with (0 0 3) at the same |Q| (1.436 Å⁻¹, same form factor) using kagome structure factors: ferromagnetic correlations give |F|² = 1 at (1 0 1) and 9 at (0 0 3), while an in-plane q = 0 120° antiferromagnetic component gives 4 and 0. Concludes that the weak (1 0 1) diffuse signal rules out strong in-plane antiferromagnetic correlations near T_C.
5. Weighs the competing explanations: a separate intermediate-temperature in-plane AFM phase (no second critical anomaly and no AFM-sensitive diffuse signal at 165 K or 180 K); domain or phase-separation effects; interlayer exchange producing AFM stacking (no half-integer-L scattering is probed, and the Hamiltonian's Jc1 is ferromagnetic). States that the data favour a single canted ferromagnetic state below T_C.
6. Reads the Hamiltonian correctly: J1 = −56 meV (FM), a DM vector with an in-plane component of 15 meV perpendicular to the bond and an out-of-plane component of 6 meV (|D|/|J1| ≈ 0.27–0.29), Jc1 = −3 meV on the 4.641 Å interlayer bond (bond 3, not the 4.639 Å in-plane shell), and S = 1 with each bond counted once. Propagates the DM matrix to the other bonds by the R-3m operations, transposing it for reversed bonds, consistent with Moriya's rules.
7. Explains that the in-plane DM component cants the moments away from c (an umbrella with a 120° in-plane pattern, tilt ≈ 8.7°), that the out-of-plane component alone would not, and that the zone-centre gap (≈ 4.2 meV) comes from the DMI alone. Notes that the model does not reproduce the measured canting (≈ 17°) or the measured gap (2–3 meV) exactly.
8. States what these data cannot determine: D/J1 cannot be extracted from the diffuse scattering itself; neither can ordered moments or canting angles be taken from these Bragg peaks; and a critical exponent has limited precision because it depends on the T_C and background choices.
