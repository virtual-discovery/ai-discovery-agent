# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Identifies `standard` as a non-magnetic PBE+SOC calculation (no starting magnetization, no magnetization in its output), whose nodes are time-reversal symmetric and lie about 39 meV above E_F, and `hubbard` as the magnetic DFT+U state (Ti and Mn moments antiparallel along z, small net moment). States that PBE and DFT+U total energies cannot be compared to decide which state is the ground state.
2. Maps DFT bands 52/53 to Wannier bands 16/17 of the DFT+U model and 18/19 of the PBE model, explaining that the DFT+U disentanglement window removes two bands beyond `exclude_bands`, and supports the mapping by matching eigenvalues.
3. Builds the census from gap minimisation plus Berry-flux chirality (±1, total zero), rejects minima with a finite gap (the ≈7 meV PBE avoided crossings), and notes that the PBE pairs (≈0.15 Å⁻¹ apart) merge on coarse grids, so that a grid alone gives the wrong count.
4. Derives the magnetic point group from the scf symmetry list (8 operations, 4 combined with time reversal), and shows that the 24 DFT+U nodes split into one orbit of 8 near k_z = 0, displaced to ±δ with the sign tied to chirality, and four orbits of 4 on the C2T-invariant planes k_x = 0 and k_y = 0; contrasts this with the single orbit of 24 of the non-magnetic cubic group.
5. Shows that the DFT+U Wannier model reproduces DFT on its 9×9×9 fitting grid but not between grid points: symmetry-equivalent nodes appear at energies tens of meV apart and the model is gapped where the archived DFT lines cross. Takes the node energy (≈ −97.5 meV), the pair separation (≈0.37 Å⁻¹) and the k_z offset from the DFT lines.
6. Classifies the nodes as type I from the velocities along all three DFT lines, places the interband (Pauli-blocking) onset near 2|E_W|, and discusses the Γ hole pocket and why the archived Kubo onset is smeared.
7. Identifies the Kubo folders as k-mesh sizes (`berry_kmesh`, fixed 2 meV smearing), checks convergence between the densest meshes, and states that σ_yz = σ_zx = 0 by symmetry for moments along z, attributing the archived nonzero values to numerical symmetry breaking of the Wannier model.
8. Notes what the data do not support: the electronic structure uses the PBE lattice parameter, at which the DFT+U run is under about 240 kbar, while the archived DFT+U vc-relax expands the primitive cell to 3.364 Å (+24 % volume), so the census at the DFT+U equilibrium volume is untested; the energies of the four k_x/k_y-plane orbits have no DFT check; Fermi-arc observability rests on the Wannier model.
