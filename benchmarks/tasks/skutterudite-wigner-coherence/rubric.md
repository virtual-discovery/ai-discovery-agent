# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Defines κ_P as the single-mode relaxation-time populations conductivity (degenerate subspaces belong to the populations) and κ_C as the coherences between non-degenerate bands with a Lorentzian overlap of full linewidths Γ = 1/τ (= 4π × phono3py gamma), and states the regime criterion: coherences matter when linewidths become comparable to interband spacings (τ ≲ 1/Δω_avg).
2. Identifies the numerically failed isotope rates in the Yb-filled runs (gamma_isotope of 10³–10⁷ THz, all at the q-point nearest Γ and including acoustic branches), explains why they are unphysical (mass-variance rates are orders of magnitude below the mode frequency and below 0.01 THz elsewhere), removes them without discarding the q-point, and quantifies the consequence (κ_P of YbIr4Sb12 about 11% higher, T_half about 60 K higher). Notes that the archived YbCo4Sb12 table reproduces only with the corrupted rates.
3. Validates the conventions against phono3py logs of released runs and resolves the inconsistent CoSb3 references: tc_data.dat gives κ_P twice the phono3py log because its post-processing script uses a populations prefactor of 0.5 instead of 0.25; the "isotope" folders of the phono3py 1.19 runs contain kappa files that are byte-identical copies of the no-isotope files, so only their logs carry the isotope results.
4. Shows that the archived gamma excludes isotope scattering, which must be added, and that it matters for the unfilled compounds at low temperature (IrSb3 κ_P at 50 K about 6% lower) while it is negligible for YbIr4Sb12 once the corrupted rates are removed.
5. Identifies Yb rattling in the harmonic data (Yb mean-square displacement several times that of the cage atoms; Yb-projected density of states concentrated in flat bands near 1 THz that hybridise with acoustic and Sb modes). Recognises that the archived projected-DOS frequency axis is inflated by about 6.97, because the PDOS runs used phonopy's QE calculator (factor 108.97) on force constants in eV/Å² (phono3py factor 15.63), and cross-checks the corrected rattler frequency against the mode frequencies or the Yb MSD. Argues that filling shortens lifetimes across the spectrum rather than only at the rattler frequency.
6. Describes the temperature trends: κ_P falls roughly as 1/T while κ_C rises and saturates; unfilled compounds stay in the Boltzmann regime (κ_C/κ_P ≤ 0.1 at 800 K), whereas Yb-filled compounds approach or reach κ_C/κ_P = 0.5, YbIr4Sb12 at about 670 K.
7. Weighs the competing explanations with evidence: purely resonant scattering (contradicted by the order-of-magnitude growth of κ_C/κ_P), a glass-like minimum-conductivity picture (κ_P still dominates below T_half), magnetism (only harmonic antiferromagnetic data are archived, with Γ frequencies close to the nonmagnetic ones) and the fc3 source (YbFe4Sb12 finite differences versus hiPhive change κ_P by a factor of about 2.4 but κ_C by about 10%). States the limits: SMA, a 9×9×9 mesh, three-phonon and isotope scattering only.
