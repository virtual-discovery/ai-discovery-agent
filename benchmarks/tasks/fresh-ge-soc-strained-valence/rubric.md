# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Explains the spin–orbit splitting of the p-like valence states at Γ into the fourfold Γ8+ (j = 3/2) and the twofold split-off Γ7+ (j = 1/2) level, and under (001) strain the heavy-hole (m_j = ±3/2) and light-hole (m_j = ±1/2) levels; states that the light hole and the split-off level share the same symmetry in the tetragonal crystal and mix, while the heavy hole does not.
2. Identifies that in PBE the s-like antibonding Γ7− (Γ2′) level lies below the p-like Γ8+ quartet at the protocol's own lattice parameter (and below the split-off level), so that the "first level above the valence-band top" or band counting does not give the s state; identifies levels by parity or symmetry labels (or by comparing spin–orbit and scalar-relativistic runs, or their different strain response) rather than by band index, and notes the consequence for the Fermi level (semimetal; the mesh band edges and the HOMO/LUMO of fixed occupations are not a gap).
3. Notes that band energies at the mesh point nearest Γ are at Γ only on a Γ-containing mesh, and that the inverted s band disperses so strongly near Γ that off-Γ values are useless for the levels.
4. Determines a0 from converged pressures of the protocol (noting the cutoff dependence of the stress), measures every strain from that a0 rather than from the experimental lattice parameter, and relaxes the out-of-plane lattice parameter (σ_zz = 0), quoting the resulting ratio −ε⊥/ε∥ = 2C12/C11 and what fixed-c or cubic cells would do to the splitting.
5. Extracts b in the small-strain limit, either from small (and preferably ± symmetric) strains or from a model that includes the light-hole/split-off coupling (6×6 Bir–Pikus), and shows that the linear formula applied at −1 % is biased because the strain splitting is not small compared with Δ0; states the sign convention of b used.
6. Locates the s/split-off crossing by tracking each level's character across strains (the two levels have opposite parity and cross without repelling), not by the order of sorted levels.
7. Compares with experiment (Δ0 ≈ 0.30 eV, E0 ≈ +0.8–0.9 eV, b ≈ −2.2 to −2.9 eV) and states limits: PBE inverts the gap (no hybrid functional or GW, unlike the paired HSE study), static lattice, bulk crystal without quantum confinement, one functional and pseudopotential; effective masses near Γ are not reliable in this inverted protocol.
