# Physical-reasoning rubric

Each item is a checkable statement that the report must make explicitly.

1. Extracts D from the linear, diffusive part of the mobile-ion MSD (after the ballistic rise and the vibrational plateau), with the time base taken from each run's input deck (UO2 saves a frame every 0.02 ps, the other systems every 0.04 ps). Handles the centre-of-mass motion: in the zero-momentum frame the immobile sublattice drifts, which is not cation diffusion. Excludes runs whose MSD never leaves the plateau.
2. Locates T_c of the 768-atom cell at the c_V maximum (about 1500 K for CaF2, 800 K for PbF2 and 2740 K for UO2), and states an uncertainty that reflects the broad CaF2 and PbF2 peaks. Shows that T_c moves up in smaller cells (by about 150 K in 96-atom CaF2 and PbF2 cells and by about 570 K in 96-atom UO2).
3. Shows that the change of activation energy at T_c appears only in cells of several hundred atoms. In the 768-atom cells the ordered regime is steeper (CaF2 about 1.9 eV, PbF2 about 0.6 eV) than the superionic regime (about 0.7 and 0.3 eV). The 96-atom cells show a single intermediate slope, or an inverted one for PbF2.
4. Reports that small cells overestimate D in the ordered regime and underestimate it near and above T_c. Uses this sign change, and the saturation of D at larger cells, to rule out a hydrodynamic (Yeh–Hummer) 1/L correction and pure statistical noise. Attributes the size effect to the collective Frenkel/order–disorder transition, which small periodic cells suppress and smear.
5. Tests whether the 768-atom cell is converged with a larger cell, in the regime where it matters. For PbF2, 1500 atoms agree with 768 atoms within about 7% at 800 K but give about 1.8 times the 768-atom D at 600 K. Predicts the 4116-atom values from the larger cell. Notes that the UO2 transition still shifts by about 200 K between 324 and 768 atoms.
6. Explains α-AgI: D is size-independent within noise from 32 to 500 atoms, with a single small activation energy (about 0.1 eV) and no c_V peak. As a type-I conductor its Ag sublattice is already molten, so no collective disordering transition exists for the cell to suppress, and no fluorite-derived size correction applies.
7. States what the data do not support: thermodynamic-limit T_c values, UO2 activation energies (ln D is curved on both sides of T_c, so the values depend on the window), and quantitative comparison with experiment for these empirical force fields.
