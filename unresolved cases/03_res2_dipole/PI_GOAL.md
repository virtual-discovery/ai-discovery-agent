# PI goal — case 03 ReS2 stacking-dependent exciton dipole (2026-10-28)

Goal: take the Zhou et al. (ACS Photonics 2021) G0W0-BSE chain for AA- and AB-stacked few-layer ReS2 (absorption along and across the Re chain; Exc. I/II at 1.46/1.50 eV for AA and 1.48/1.52 eV for AB), rebuild it in QE + BerkeleyGW with slab truncation, add the exciton charge distribution across layers (layer-resolved electron and hole weights from the BSE eigenvectors, giving the dipole length d = ⟨z_e − z_h⟩), a computed out-of-plane dielectric constant, and a symmetry test of both stackings, and refine it into one first-principles prediction of the linear Stark shift per stacking. The gated Stark shift is treated as a probe of the exciton's permanent out-of-plane dipole. Plotted against field, the predicted shift must vanish for AA by symmetry and reproduce the AB value at the device field. Experimental anchors: AB ≈ 13 meV (linear plus quadratic, off-state), AA negligible, 6–8 layers; the gate field, thickness and exciton label are taken from the Paul paper at stage 0. GPUs may be used, and speed comes before cost.

1. Reproduce the Li before production: Exc. I and II energies within 0.15 eV for both stackings, their polarization, and the sign of the AB−AA difference.
2. Symmetry and null: the relaxed AA slab must contain a layer-exchange operation and the AB slab must not (spglib, 1e-3 Å); if AB also has one the hypothesis is dead at the structural level. In AA, |d| for Exc. I and II must be at most 0.05 Å; a larger value is a code defect and blocks the campaign.
3. Dipole, magnitude and identity: in AB, |d| of the measured exciton (named by energy and polarization) must be at least 0.20 Å, and the predicted linear shift e·d·F_ext/ε_⊥ at the paper's maximal field must lie within a factor of two of the measured linear part, with ε_⊥ computed, not taken from the literature. d must change by at most 0.10 Å between two k-levels and two vacuum sizes.
4. Extended: a four-layer AB slab (interior versus surface excitons) and a fully-relativistic AB bilayer as the SOC covariate.
5. Optional: the quadratic part from a two-field sawtooth GW-BSE on AA (exciton polarizability).

Item 2 is a symmetry-protected null and item 3 the positive, so the character measure cannot pass vacuously. The 6–8-layer sample is declared a limitation. If the device field cannot be obtained, the campaign stops at stage 0 with the dipole lengths reported and no magnitude verdict.
