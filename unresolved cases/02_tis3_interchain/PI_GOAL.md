# PI goal — case 02 TiS3 interchain exciton (2026-10-28)

Goal: take the Molina-Mendoza et al. (2015) bulk TiS3 G0W0+BSE chain (Yambo, plasmon pole, 10×10×10 k, 30 valence × 70 conduction bands; E_QP 1.15, E_opt 1.05, E_b 0.10 eV, with a second feature near 1.2–1.3 eV), rebuild it in QE + BerkeleyGW, add polarization-resolved channels (E∥b, E∥a, E∥c*), exciton eigenvectors with real-space conditional densities for a hole fixed on one chain, and a slab-truncated monolayer control, and refine it into one first-principles polarization-resolved absorption spectrum of bulk TiS3. Transmission is treated as a probe of bound excitons. Plotted against the measured E∥b and E⊥b traces at the same broadening, the spectrum must show the E∥b edge exciton and, in the ⊥-chain channel only, the 1.28 eV peak, and the maps must say whether that state is interchain. Experimental anchors: 1.28 eV peak for E⊥b below 150 K, optical gap 1.07 eV, STS gap 1.2 eV, binding 0.13 eV; the per-polarization edge energies, the 1.28 eV linewidth and the measured crystal face are frozen from the Boldyrev figures at stage 0. GPUs may be used, and speed comes before cost.

1. Reproduce the Li before production: E_QP, E_opt and E_b within 0.10 eV and the second feature present.
2. Positions after one global shift: one shift of at most 0.15 eV fixed by the E∥b edge exciton and applied to every channel. Afterwards the E∥a channel must have a local maximum in 1.18–1.38 eV at σ = 0.03 eV with prominence at least 20 % of the channel maximum in 0.9–1.8 eV and an a/b intensity ratio of at least 3 at that energy. Independently, E_QP within 0.25 eV of 1.2 and E_b within 0.07 eV of 0.13, reported separately.
3. Identity and character: the leading state must be bound (E_b ≥ 0.03 eV) and distinct from the edge exciton (overlap ≤ 0.1), and no two experimental features may share one theoretical state. With the hole on a Ti site and separately on an S site, the electron weight on other chains of the same layer must be at least 0.50 for interchain, at most 0.25 for intrachain, otherwise mixed; both hole sites must agree, and the edge exciton must score at most 0.35 by the same measure. Maps on the Born-von-Karman box with the containment check.
4. Extended: the monolayer control must run before the bulk verdict; the statement that dimensionality explains the earlier monolayer polarization trend is allowed only if the bulk and monolayer a−b exciton splittings differ by at least 0.10 eV.
5. Optional: assign the 1.29 eV photocurrent transition of Molina-Mendoza et al. to the same state and predict its polarization.

Items 2 and 3 use the round-8 tolerances for one global shift and width-scaled windows, the round-8 real-space rules, and the grid gates from the dense-kernel work: positions within 0.03 eV and strengths within 25 % between two k-levels, kernel window ×1.5 moving the peak by at most 0.03 eV. The 150 K onset is declared not predicted.
