# PI goal — case 01 ZrSe3 strain (2026-10-28)

Goal: take the Li et al. (Adv. Mater. 2022) bulk ZrSe3 G0W0+BSE chain (Yambo, plasmon pole, 6×6×6 k, uniaxial +1 % along a and b), rebuild it in QE + BerkeleyGW, add biaxial in-plane strain with a declared relaxation rule (a and b scaled together; c, β and internal coordinates relaxed with PBE-D3) and the BSE binding energy at every strain, and refine it into one first-principles decomposition E_opt(ε) = E_QP(ε) − E_b(ε) of the A exciton. Differential reflectance is treated as a probe of the first bright E∥b exciton. Plotted against the measured gauge factor, the computed E_opt, E_QP and E_b lines must reproduce the redshift under compression and say which term carries it. Experimental anchors: gauge 136 meV/% under biaxial compression, A exciton 1.75 eV, A_2s 1.89 eV; published uniaxial quasiparticle shifts +90 (b) and +45 (a) meV/%; measured uniaxial exciton gauges 60–95 (b) and 0–15 (a) meV/%. GPUs may be used, and speed comes before cost.

1. Reproduce the Li before production: uniaxial +1 % quasiparticle shifts within 30 meV of +90 and +45 meV/%. A miss on either stops the campaign.
2. Gauge factor: one linear fit over ε ∈ {−1.0, −0.5, 0, +0.5} %, residual at most 10 meV, slope between 109 and 218 meV/% (0.8× to 1.6× measured; the lower bound is tight because partial strain transfer only lowers the measured value). No parameter may be adjusted to reach 136.
3. Decomposition and identity: E_QP and E_b reported separately at every point, never only their difference. QP share at least 0.70 is QP-driven, at most 0.30 is binding-driven, otherwise mixed. The A exciton must be the same leading E∥b stick at every strain, and both slopes must change by at most 15 meV/% between two k-grid levels.
4. Extended: a fully-relativistic chain at 0 and −1 % (SOC becomes primary if the local slope moves more than 20 meV/%), and the uniaxial exciton gauges compared with the measured 60–95 and 0–15 meV/% ranges.
5. Optional: unstrained E_b against the A_2s−A_1s spacing, and the intrinsic gauge stated as an upper bound on the strain transfer of the few-layer sample.

Items 2 and 3 are derivative tolerances; round 8 (RuCl3) showed spacings converge an order of magnitude before absolute GW-BSE energies, and the Li's own absolute gap sits 0.46 eV below the measured exciton. The additivity prior of 135 meV/% is written into the card before any number is computed.
